Design cards bind photos, live stock and orders

Solution flow — before the working demo

Today photos sit in tablet folders, stock sits somewhere else, and orders happen on WhatsApp. The customer asks for a design and availability; the salesperson hunts folders, then types stock by hand. The Design Card replaces that. One record holds the gallery, the stock matrix, the share pack, and the order line.

1

Capture into a design

Studio shoots on the tablet straight into TG-2148 — not into a folder named “floral”. Each photo is tagged with view and colorway.

2

Stock rides with the card

Warehouse keeps a color × size matrix on the same design. Sales never retypes “we have 426 pcs”.

3

Customer asks → share pack

Search the design, pick photos, send WhatsApp with images + live stock + rate in one tap.

4

Take the order here

Same screen: customer, color, size, qty. Stock is checked and reserved. Order number goes back to the customer.

Folder on the tablet

  • Photos have no design code
  • Stock is a second conversation
  • Order is a third chat / paper slip
  • New staff cannot find “that floral”

Design Card

  • Every photo belongs to a SKU
  • Availability travels with the image
  • Order is a button on the same card
  • Search by code, name, fabric, color

Tap the shutter to attach a studio shot to the selected design. On a real tablet this uses the camera; in the demo it stamps a labelled still onto the Design Card. You can also upload a photo.
